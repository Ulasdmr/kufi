# Kufi: Project Details & Initial Research

## 1. Problem Statement
Finding dependable grooming services in urban areas is often inefficient and fragmented. Customers struggle to discover barbers and salons that match their location, pricing expectations, and stylistic preferences. Kufi centralizes local discovery and provides a transparent, synchronized booking workflow.

## 2. Preliminary Scope (MVP)
### In Scope:
* User registration and role-based authentication (Client & Salon Owner)
* Salon search and district-based filtering
* Service catalog and pricing management
* Real-time appointment scheduling and slot reservation

### Out of Scope:
* Integrated online payment processing
* AI-based hairstyle recommendation
* In-app instant messaging
